# Slightly stronger/faster trucks and occasional falling objects

Owner requested a small power/speed improvement and intermittent objects trying to hit the car after a certain distance. Selected threshold: 300 meters, leaving early ramps clear. Implementation: +7% motor force, +8% motor speed ceiling, +10% chassis impact resistance. Pitch damping tuned to keep early landings controllable; top-speed gain is a configuration change, not a guarantee of an 8% faster completion for every build.

One bounded sky object at a time: wooden crate, rounded rock, or crystal meteor. Simulation owns a fixed predicted target, a 1.6-second advance warning, swept collision, one-impact damage and cleanup. Nothing homes after the warning. One new distance threshold at least 100–160 meters after the last spawn, plus an 8-second quiet interval after the previous impact has cleared; defer while airborne, stopped, overturned or targeting steep ground. Actual intervals can be longer. No drops in practice. No catch-up barrage or repeated same-distance spawning. The target arrow and landing shadow remain readable on phones, including an edge cue for a target not yet onscreen.

Direct impacts slow/jolt the car and may detach one actual equipped piece; the first hits remove roof/accessory pieces before wheels. Stronger structures resist more. Impacts do not delete owned parts, cost repair bolts, or create a new reward currency. Existing crashes, turbo, quick retry, personal bests, authored terrain, prices, 100-part inventory, reward formula, unicorn art/gameplay and parent profile version remain intact. Optional bounded sky snapshot fields preserve the warning, target, outcome and next spawn threshold on reload. Legacy long-distance saves get an 80-meter grace stretch.
