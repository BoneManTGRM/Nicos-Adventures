# Nico's World Art Bible

## Canonical direction

Nico's World uses premium illustrated 2D/2.5D storybook realism: believable anatomy, materials, light, shadow, depth, perspective, and environmental integration without uncanny photorealism.

All new player-visible production art is authored as polished 2D illustration. Layered 2.5D depth, skeletal rigs, mesh deformation, and compositing are allowed when they preserve the illustrated result. Do not expand the product with new 3D destinations or make new art depend on a 3D runtime. Existing 3D surfaces may remain only as a temporary strangler-migration path until their 2D replacements pass feature, accessibility, persistence, performance, and browser parity.

The approved Nico reference sheets and the premium broken/restored World Map establish the finish bar. Reject flat clip-art treatment, emoji as finished character art, heavy generic cartoon outlines, pasted-on characters, toy-plastic rendering, and effects that substitute glow for material definition.

The minimum professional bar is:

- clear foreground, midground, and background separation;
- one coherent key light with supporting ambient and rim light;
- contact shadows and believable grounding;
- material-specific highlights, texture, and edge treatment;
- consistent perspective, scale, and color script within a destination;
- expressive, child-friendly silhouettes without anatomical or facial distortion;
- restrained detail that remains readable on an iPhone screen.

## Canonical Nico

The approved illustrated Nico references are the visual source of truth. Production assets must preserve:

- dark textured hair;
- round red glasses;
- expressive dark eyes;
- warm stylized facial proportions;
- consistent apparent age and silhouette;
- green/cream identity;
- khaki explorer styling;
- green sneakers;
- curious, smart, kind, adventurous character.

The private family photograph is reference-only and must never be committed, cached, bundled, or shipped.

## Consistency gate

Reject production assets that materially drift in face, glasses, hair, eye appearance, body proportions, apparent age, skin tone, silhouette, or wardrobe geometry.

## Rendering

- coherent physically believable lighting;
- contact shadows;
- environment color influence;
- dimensional foreground/midground/background composition;
- restrained atmospheric effects;
- readable child-friendly silhouettes;
- consistent camera scale and perspective.

Characters must look present in the environment, not pasted over it.

## Asset contract

Production assets should use an appropriate web format: WebP/AVIF for painted environments and opaque character frames, PNG/WebP when raster transparency is required, and SVG only for controlled interface illustration that meets the same finish bar. Assets require documented provenance, deterministic optimization, responsive sizing, stable anchors, and explicit state identity.

Nico, BoltBot, creatures, and wardrobe pieces must remain layerable or riggable wherever motion, equipment, expression, or outfit changes require it. Character sets share one approved scale, bottom-center anchor, silhouette, lighting model, and palette. Individual poses or outfits must not be generated as unrelated one-off characters.

The current GLB assets are transitional compatibility artifacts, not visual authority and not the foundation for new work. Preserve them until the matching 2D path passes parity; remove them only after the canonical 2D replacement is production-proven.

An asset becomes production-approved only after representative desktop and iPhone/WebKit reviews confirm its front, three-quarter, and side identity where applicable, material definition, motion, environment integration, crop safety, and loading behavior.

## Performance

Visual fidelity may scale by device quality tier, but character identity, gameplay readability, and interaction semantics must not change.

## Dinosaur Valley

The Sunrise Overlook establishes the destination's environment language: warm early light, cool atmospheric depth, layered rock and plant silhouettes, readable river reflections, and restrained storybook color. Dinosaurs use believable species silhouettes and grounded contact shadows without chasing photorealism. Future authored 2D creatures must preserve the same scale, framing, accessibility, and mobile performance contract.
